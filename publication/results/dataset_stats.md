### 3.1 Cross-Corpus Differences

| Aspect | FAERS D1 | VAERS |
|---|---|---|
| Source | FDA drug ADR reports | US vaccine AEFI reports |
| Primary entity focus | Drugs & ADRs | Vaccines & symptoms |
| Annotation scheme emphasis | Drug roles (suspect/concomitant), dose | Symptom/AE, diagnosis levels |
| AE term diversity (unique) | 3,991 | 13,819 |
| Drug/vaccine diversity (unique) | 2,085 | 863 |
| Avg doc length (tokens) | 500 | 440 |
| Length variability (std dev) | 255 | 143 |

FAERS D1 reports are more heterogeneous in length (σ = 255 tokens) reflecting varied
clinical narrative complexity, while VAERS reports are more uniform (σ = 143 tokens).
VAERS exhibits substantially higher AE-term diversity (13,819 unique terms vs. 3,991),
driven by highly individualised symptom descriptions across thousands of vaccine types
and patient populations. Conversely, FAERS D1 has richer drug-entity variety (2,085
unique terms vs. 863 in VAERS), consistent with its broader pharmaceutical scope.

## 4. Notes on Annotation Layers

Each JSON file may contain annotations from multiple sources identified by the `note` field:

| `note` value | Source |
|---|---|
| `SME1` | Primary expert annotator (human, used for gold standard) |
| `LLM` | Large language model predictions |
| `ETHER` | ETHER system predictions |

All statistics in this report are computed exclusively from **SME1 (expert) annotations**,
which constitute the gold-standard labels used for model training and evaluation.
